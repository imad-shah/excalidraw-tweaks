Excalidraw Tweaks

Toolbar & shortcuts

Your Excalidraw toolbar. Drag to reorder, double-click to hide or show, click to change a shortcut. Crossed-out tools are hidden on the site. Shortcuts are comma-separated, e.g. p, 3 or alt+d. A default key you take away from a tool is blocked, so it won't fall back to it.

Quick-pick colors

These replace the row of swatches beside Stroke and Background. Five fit per row; add more and they wrap onto extra rows. Click a swatch to open the color spectrum (hover to see the hex, click to use it), or type a hex code (transparent works for background).

Interface

Keep Excalidraw in light mode (so your colors are exact) and give the toolbar and panels a dark look anyway. Set the canvas color itself from Excalidraw's ☰ menu.

Panel color

Color hotkeys

Hold these modifiers and press 1–9 (0 = 10th) to pick that quick-pick color. It applies to anything selected and to what you draw next. Leave empty to turn off.

Share & back up

Export saves everything above (toolbar order, hidden and pinned tools, shortcuts, colors, color hotkeys, interface) to one file. Import that file on another computer, or send it to someone, to get the exact same setup.

Reset

Back to stock Excalidraw: every tool shown in its original order, original shortcuts, original colors.
